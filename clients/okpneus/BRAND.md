# Brand — OK Pneus Terrebonne

Independent shop in the OK Tire network (Terrebonne, QC). Owner: Sam Singh.
Derived 2026-11-11; accent switched from red to yellow to match existing OK Pneus visuals (Pam, 2026-11-11). No official
brand kit or logo file in this repo yet — replace the text wordmark with the real
logo when available.

## Colors
- Primary:   #FFC20E (jaune — accents, blocs CTA, tags; texte noir sur fond jaune). Approximation : remplacer par le hex exact des autres visuels.
- Ink:       #111111 (near-black — backgrounds, body text)
- Surface:   #FFFFFF (white — text on dark, light slides)
- Neutral:   #F2F2F2 / #8A8A8A (light panels, secondary text)
- Restraint: yellow is the only accent; no other hues. Never yellow text on white.

## Typography
- Headline: Oswald 600/700, uppercase, condensed (Google Fonts)
- Body: Inter 400/600/800

## Logo
- Until a logo file is supplied: wordmark "OK PNEUS" (Oswald 700, black on yellow badge; yellow on black badge when the background is yellow)
  + "TERREBONNE" (Inter 600, letter-spaced). Placement: top-left or bottom, clear space = badge height.

## Voice & tone
- In 3 words: direct, local, rassurant
- Do: tutoiement on IG/FB, Quebec French ("char", "checker"), practical safety tips
- Don't: fear-mongering, fake urgency, prices/dates not confirmed by Sam

## Default on-image language
- French (Québec). English quotes from press get translated, attribution kept.

## Audience
- Drivers and small fleets in Terrebonne, Mascouche, Repentigny (Lanaudière / Rive-Nord)

## Standing conversion objective
- Leads: appointments (phone / DM / booking link)

## Visual do/don't
- Imagery: real shop photos, tires, tread close-ups; bold flat yellow/black graphics
- Avoid: generic stock smiles, snowflake clip-art overload
- Approvals: every post goes through Sam ("Approbation Sam" in Airtable calendar appzNmBQeCXPUVnff)
