## 2. Audience (inferred, ⚠️ TO CONFIRM)

- Primary: Afro-descendant women in Quebec, especially Montréal and the South
  Shore. They want clean, plant-based ingredients and struggle with dry skin
  and hair in winter.
- The site says skin and hair need a *« climat chaud et humide »* to glow,
  hence the winter-dryness angle.
- Secondary: men (one blog post speaks to them). Ships Canada-wide.

## 4. Voice

- **Three words (proposed):** naturelle · bienveillante · experte
  (natural · caring · knowledgeable).
- **Register:** *vous* (matches the site). Warm, reassuring, concrete. Teach
  simple rituals; no hype.
- **Language: FR-first bilingual, a hard rule.** Every caption and every piece
  of on-image text is written in French first, then English. The two versions
  must say the same thing; neither is a stub.

### Never say / claims discipline

- ❌ No medical or cure claims (no "treats eczema", "heals", "cures").
- ❌ **« stimule la croissance des cheveux »** (from the blog): don't reuse
  until Miboty substantiates it. No biotin-growth claims.
- ⚠️ **« sans perturbateurs endocriniens »**: use only once Miboty provides
  substantiation (Onboarding item).
- ❌ Don't invent ingredient benefits. Describe origin, texture and how to use
  it; benefit claims need CUMBA's approval and the product's ingredient (INCI) list.
- ❌ Don't invent promos, discounts or deadlines. Use a placeholder
  `[OFFRE À CONFIRMER PAR CUMBA]` until confirmed.
- No social handles are known yet, so never tag or invent one.

## 7. Imagery rules

- Real Miboty product photos and video only. No stock models, no AI-generated
  people, no fake before/afters.
- Customer photos and testimonials **only with written consent**.
- Every post that needs a real asset is flagged `ASSET NEEDED: …` in the
  Airtable `Contenu` field. Attachments need public URLs, so client files are a
  manual upload (`Fichier Client (ajouté par)` or `Visuels`).

## 8. Platforms & cadence

- **Instagram, TikTok, Facebook only.**
- **~20 posts / month (5 / week)** across the three, each beat re-cut per
  platform. November 2026: 10 Instagram, 5 TikTok, 5 Facebook.

## 9. Validation workflow

1. TIC drafts each post in Airtable → `Statut production = À produire`,
   `Validation CUMBA = À valider`.
2. CUMBA reviews on **techinnovation.consulting/validation?token=…**, choosing
   **Approuvé** or **À ajuster** with a comment.
3. TIC fixes, produces the visuals, then schedules the post → `Statut production
   = Programmé`. The post then drops out of the validation page automatically.
   After publishing → `Publié` + `Lien publié`.

Nothing is ever created as approved or published.
