# Partner outreach: installers and affiliate programs

## Who qualifies as a partner

- Active **RBQ licence** with the right subclass (verify on the RBQ registry), NEQ in good standing
- Installs that qualify for **LogisVert** (they handle the Hydro-Québec connection request)
- 4.3★+ on Google with 20+ reviews; no OPC enforcement history
- Agrees in writing to our referral terms below

Target: 3 active partners covering Montréal/Laval, the Shores, and Québec City
before December 4; then 1–2 per added region.

## Referral terms to put in the agreement (have a lawyer finalise)

1. **Fee:** $___ per signed residential contract (target $500–$1,000) or __ % of the
   pre-tax contract value; commercial by quote. Due within 30 days of installation.
2. **Attribution:** a lead is ours for 12 months from the day we send it.
3. **Reporting:** partner updates the lead's status (visit, quote, signed,
   installed, lost) within 5 business days, by reply email or shared Airtable form.
4. **Conduct:** no unsolicited home visits; first contact by phone or email only;
   no pressure tactics; respect the 10-day cancellation right; no use of
   Hydro-Québec's name or logo to imply affiliation.
5. **Privacy:** leads are used only to quote that customer; no resale; deletion on request (Law 25).
6. **Exclusivity:** none. We send each lead to at most 3 partners.
7. **Termination:** 30 days' notice; fees owed on leads already sent stay owed.

## Affiliate programs to apply to (week 2)

Apply only where the product genuinely fits the audience, and disclose every link.

- Amazon.ca Associates: portable power stations, balcony kits, EV chargers, snow brushes for panels
- Portable power-station brands with Canadian affiliate programs (compare commission rates and cookie length on each brand's site before applying)
- EV charger installers or retailers in Quebec, if they offer referral fees

Log every program in Airtable › Partenaires with Type = « Programme d'affiliation (produits) ».
