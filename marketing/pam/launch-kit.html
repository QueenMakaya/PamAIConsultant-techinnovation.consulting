Launch PAM, step by step

  1. Set up the basics 2 days

    • Share the offer page (Share menu, anyone with the link) and add that link to every bio. It opens in French or English by itself; add #fr or #en to force one.
    • Create a free 20-minute booking link (Calendly free plan, or Google Calendar appointment schedule) named “PAM: appel découverte / discovery call”.
    • Save the reply for “PAM” messages below.
    • List 20 people you've helped or met, and 5 organizations (incubators, chambers of commerce, entrepreneurship programs).

  2. Refresh your profiles 1 day

    • LinkedIn: banner, new headline, a 3-line About, and the offer page in Featured.
    • Instagram: switch to a free professional account, update the bio and link.
    • Facebook: turn on Professional mode on your personal profile, add the cover, bio and link.
    • Then comment on other people's posts for 15 minutes a day for a week before you post.

  3. Post the 3-week plan weeks 1–3

    • Post on LinkedIn first, then the same day on Instagram and Facebook. Meta Business Suite publishes to both in one go.
    • Stories go on Instagram and Facebook. Answer every comment the same day.

  4. Make the ask week 3

    • Publish the offer post, then send personal messages to your 20 people that week.
    • Pitch a cohort pilot to 2 of your 5 organizations.

  5. Count what happened weeks 4–6

    • Track four numbers: “PAM” messages, calls booked, diagnostics sold, Co-Founder 90 signed.
    • Target: 3 paying founders and 1 organization pilot. That's the signal to keep going.

What actually brings the first clients

  • Message 20 people you've already helped or met, one by one, the week the offer goes live. Posts warm them up; direct messages get the yes.
